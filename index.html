BMI Calculator Online

Check your Body Mass Index quickly. BMI is a widely used measure to assess if you're at a healthy weight for your height.

Understanding BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by your height in meters squared. It provides a quick assessment of body fat based on height and weight.

BMI Categories:

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obese: BMI 30 or greater

Body Fat Calculator Online

Measure your body fat percentage easily using the U.S. Navy method.

Body Fat Categories

Men

  • Essential fat: 2-5%
  • Athletes: 6-13%
  • Fitness: 14-17%
  • Average: 18-24%
  • Obese: 25%+

Women

  • Essential fat: 10-13%
  • Athletes: 14-20%
  • Fitness: 21-24%
  • Average: 25-31%
  • Obese: 32%+
